Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Introduction

At HD Careers, accessible at hdcareers.in, protecting visitors’ privacy is an important part of operating our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you browse job listings, use our resume matching tool, contact us, or follow links to other services.

This policy covers HD Careers’ online activities. It does not govern information you submit directly to employers, recruitment platforms, or social networks. If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, please contact us using the details below.

Consent and your choices

Please read this policy before providing personal information. Where consent is required for optional cookies or personal data processing, we will request it separately. Simply visiting this page does not replace those consent requirements. You can choose whether to use the resume matcher, contact us, or join our external job-alert channels.

Information we collect

You can browse public job listings and use search and category filters without creating a candidate account. We do not ask you to register or provide your address or telephone number to view openings.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the name you provide, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. Please provide only information needed for your enquiry; avoid sending identity documents, bank details, passwords, or other sensitive information.

Technical information may also be processed when your browser requests website pages or supporting resources, as explained below.

How we use your information

We use information relevant to our services to:

  • Operate and maintain the website and its job information features.
  • Respond to questions, privacy requests, and reports about inaccurate or expired listings.
  • Understand aggregate traffic and improve navigation and content.
  • Diagnose technical problems and protect the website against misuse.
  • Maintain records needed to resolve enquiries and meet applicable obligations.

Contacting us does not automatically subscribe you to promotional emails. Our public job-alert channels are optional and operate on the platforms you choose to join.

Resume and job description matching

Our Resume ↔ Job Description Match tool reads supported PDF, DOCX, or TXT files in your browser. The matching code processes the selected file and extracted text locally; it does not upload these contents to HD Careers’ servers.

The tool compares detected skills, relevant employment dates, and qualifications with the displayed job requirements. It shows component scores and an overall estimate. These results are not an employer’s ATS score, an application submission, or a hiring decision.

You can inspect and edit the extracted text in the page. The tool does not save your resume in an HD Careers candidate account. Avoid using it on shared devices where another person could view an open page. Files you separately email to us are handled as correspondence, not as local matcher input.

Log files and technical information

Hosting and security providers may process IP addresses, browser and device information, requested URLs, referring pages, timestamps, and error logs when serving the website. These records support delivery, troubleshooting, and security. Some technical information can qualify as personal data under applicable law.

Pages also load resources such as styles, icons, company logos, and document-reading libraries from external providers. Requests to those providers may expose connection information such as your IP address and browser details. Loading a resume-reading library is separate from uploading your resume contents.

Website traffic analytics

HD Careers uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand website usage such as page views, sessions, approximate location, traffic sources, and device categories. We also use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with pages through aggregated interaction data such as clicks, scrolling, heatmaps, and session recordings where supported.

These analytics services are operated by Google and Microsoft under their respective privacy terms. HD Careers does not intentionally send resume text, uploaded resume contents, or resume-match results as analytics events. You can learn more through Google's Privacy Policy and Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. Similar technologies can support preferences, authentication, measurement, or advertising. Our restricted administrator area may use authentication cookies; browsing public listings does not require a candidate login.

You can manage or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some technologies may affect features that depend on them. Cookie choices on external recruitment and social platforms are controlled through those services. Where optional technologies require consent, the applicable controls must be offered before those technologies are used.

Google advertising and advertising partners

If Google AdSense is enabled on HD Careers, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor’s previous visits to this website or other websites. Google’s advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits across the internet.

You can manage personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center. Learn more through Google’s advertising information and how Google uses information from partner sites.

Advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, and similar technologies to deliver and measure ads. This conditional disclosure does not mean advertisements are currently enabled. We will update this policy when additional advertising partners or materially different practices are introduced.

Third-party privacy policies

Apply buttons take you to employer career sites or recruitment platforms. Information entered there, including your resume and application answers, is handled under that service’s privacy policy. HD Careers does not receive your application merely because you followed our link.

WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, and other linked services have their own privacy settings and rules. Joining a channel or interacting on those platforms may reveal information to the platform or other participants according to its settings. Review the relevant policy before sharing information.

Retention, service providers, and security

We retain correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to answer enquiries, resolve issues, and meet applicable obligations. Retention depends on the purpose and any legal requirements. Technical logs and analytics records are subject to the relevant provider’s retention settings and policies.

Service providers supporting hosting, email, security, and enabled analytics may process information for these purposes, including in countries outside your own. We may disclose information where legally required. We take reasonable precautions, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal information, obtain a portable copy, or withdraw consent. You may also have the right to complain to the relevant data protection authority.

Where GDPR applies, access requests are normally free, subject to limited legal exceptions, and requests are generally addressed within one month, with extensions where permitted. Where CCPA applies, eligible California consumers may have rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing; access and deletion requests generally have a 45-day response period, subject to permitted extensions.

Contact us to exercise applicable rights. We may need proportionate information to verify your request. We cannot retrieve a resume that was only processed locally in your browser.

Children’s information

HD Careers provides employment and career information and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information through correspondence. Parents or guardians who believe a child has sent us personal information should contact us so we can review it and take appropriate action. Additional protections may apply to children under local law.

Policy updates and contact

We may update this policy when our features, service providers, or legal obligations change. The revision date will appear at the top of this page; material changes requiring further notice or consent will be handled accordingly.

For privacy questions, correction requests, or concerns, email helpdeskinreallife@gmail.com or visit our Contact page. Please describe your request without including unnecessary sensitive information.

Thank you for visiting HD Careers.
