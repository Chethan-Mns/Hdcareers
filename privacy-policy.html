Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how HD Careers may handle information when you visit hdcareers.in. HD Careers does not currently require visitors to create a user account to browse public job listings.

Technical information

Our hosting, security, analytics or similar service providers may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, requested URLs, timestamps and diagnostic logs. This information can be used to operate, secure, troubleshoot and understand use of the website.

Cookies and advertising

HD Careers may use cookies or similar technologies for site functionality, measurement and advertising. If Google AdSense or other Google advertising services are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or other identifiers to serve, personalize, limit and measure ads in accordance with Google's policies. Where required, visitors may be shown consent controls before advertising cookies or similar technologies are used.

You can learn about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services at Google's partner sites information page.

Resume Match tool

The Resume ↔ Job Description Match tool is designed to process supported resume files locally in your browser. HD Careers does not intentionally upload or store the resume file or extracted resume text on its servers when you use this tool. The match result is an estimate based on the job information displayed on the page and is not an employer ATS score, hiring decision or guarantee of selection.

The browser may load third-party JavaScript libraries used to read PDF or DOCX files, but the HD Careers matching logic is intended to keep the contents of the selected resume in the user's browser session.

Third-party links

Job pages link to employer career sites and other authorized recruitment platforms. Those websites have their own privacy practices. HD Careers does not control information you submit after leaving hdcareers.in.

Contact and corrections

If you email us, we receive the information contained in your message so we can respond. Please do not send sensitive personal or financial information. Privacy questions can be sent to helpdeskinreallife@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website, advertising setup or applicable requirements change. The latest revision date is shown at the top of this page.
