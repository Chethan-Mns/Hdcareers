Team access

Give each colleague a separate account. Job Editors can manage jobs, but cannot manage other users or access private analytics.

Add Job Editor

Choose a unique username and a strong temporary password. Share the password privately; it is never stored in plaintext.

Owner login stays as it is. Editor passwords are not added to Vercel as separate environment variables.

Permissions

Owner — all admin features, job publishing, analytics, team accounts, session revocation and audit logs.

Job Editor — view/create/review/publish jobs, prepare daily batches and manually run the expired-job checker. No access to team accounts, account secrets, traffic analytics or security settings.

Disabling an editor or revoking sessions takes effect across all devices on the next authenticated request. The Owner account is protected from team-level changes.

Change Owner Password

Update your own admin password without changing Vercel settings. Your current password is required. Your new password is stored securely as a salted hash; all Owner sessions will be signed out.

Owner only

After saving, all Owner devices will be signed out. Job Editor accounts and scheduled jobs are unaffected.
