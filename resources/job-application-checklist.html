Job Application Checklist Before You Submit

A five-minute review can prevent avoidable mistakes such as applying to an expired role, uploading the wrong resume or missing a required field.

By HD Careers Editorial TeamUpdated 3 Oct 20266 min read

Before opening the form

  1. Confirm the role is still active on the employer or authorized portal.
  2. Re-check location, work mode, experience and qualification requirements.
  3. Note the requisition ID and deadline when available.
  4. Prepare the version of your resume that best reflects relevant, truthful evidence.

Before clicking Submit

  1. Contact details.
    Check phone number, email and spelling.
  2. Dates.
    Make sure education and employment dates match your resume and supporting records.
  3. Documents.
    Verify that you uploaded the intended file, not an older draft.
  4. Eligibility questions.
    Answer based on the employer's wording, not on what another job board says.
  5. Consent and declarations.
    Read them before agreeing.

After submission

Save the application confirmation, requisition ID and any candidate-portal login details securely. Do not share passwords or OTPs with recruiters or third parties.

HD Careers does not submit applications on your behalf from a job page. The Official Apply button takes you to the employer or authorized recruitment destination so you can complete the process yourself.
