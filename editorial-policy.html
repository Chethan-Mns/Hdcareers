How HD Careers Verifies Job Information

Our workflow combines official source links, structured extraction, validation rules, freshness checks and corrections. Automation helps organize information, but it does not replace the employer's official posting as the final source.

1Official SourceWe start from an employer or authorized recruitment source.
2Extract FactsParser organizes title, location, eligibility, job ID, skills and links.
3Validation RulesRequired fields and source details are checked before publication.
4Link CheckThe application route is checked against the source available to us.
5PublishThe page clearly identifies HD Careers as independent from the employer.
6Freshness CheckScheduled checks look for closures, redirects or availability changes.
7Correction / ExpiryClosed jobs can be marked expired; reported errors are reviewed.

What we verify

  • Company/employer named on the source
  • Role title and location
  • Published eligibility and experience
  • Job or requisition ID when available
  • Official or authorized application destination
  • Source and verification date

What we do not claim

  • We do not guarantee job availability after the verification time.
  • We do not guarantee selection, interview or an offer.
  • We do not invent salary, batch eligibility or experience requirements when the source does not support them.
  • We do not treat the Resume Matcher as an employer ATS score.
  • We do not accept recruitment payments from candidates for applications linked on HD Careers.
Automation disclosure
Automated tools may help extract job facts, run availability checks and generate structured pages. We use validation rules, source references and correction workflows because automation can miss context or become outdated.
Corrections
If a listing contains an inaccurate detail or expired link, send the HD Careers page URL and the issue through our Contact page. We can review the source and update or expire the listing.

Why we keep source and verification information visible

A job page is time-sensitive. Showing the source and the date of the latest check helps visitors distinguish a recent verification from a permanent guarantee. The employer's page remains the final source for application status, deadlines and eligibility.

Original editorial content

HD Careers also publishes career guides and aggregate hiring insights that are created from our own verification workflow and active listings database. These pages are separated from employer job descriptions so readers can tell the difference between sourced vacancy facts and HD Careers analysis or guidance.
