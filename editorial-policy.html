How HD Careers verifies job information

We start with an employer or authorized recruitment source, organize the facts into a consistent format, run validation and freshness checks, and keep a correction path open when information changes.

Official source

We begin with an employer careers page or an authorized recruitment destination and keep that source attached to the listing.

✓
What we verify

Facts we try to trace back to the source

  • Company or employer named on the source
  • Role title and location
  • Published eligibility and experience requirements
  • Job or requisition ID when available
  • Official or authorized application destination
  • Source name and latest verification date
—
What we do not infer

Claims we avoid making without source support

  • Guaranteed availability after the latest check
  • Guaranteed interview, selection or offer
  • Salary, batch or experience requirements not stated by the source
  • An employer ATS score from our Resume Matcher
  • Recruitment payment requests on behalf of listed employers
⌁
Technology-assisted

Automation supports the workflow

Tools can help extract structured facts, generate consistent page layouts and run scheduled availability checks. We do not treat automation output as an authority by itself; the source remains the reference point and validation/correction controls stay in place.

↻
Freshness

Verification is a point-in-time check

A job can close after we verify it. That is why job pages show a latest verification date and why candidates should re-check the employer page before submitting an application.

Readers can report a problem

If you find an expired link or inaccurate detail, send us the HD Careers page URL and the issue. We can compare it with the source and update or expire the listing.

Common questions

What “verified” means on HD Careers

Does technology assistance mean the employer source is optional?+

No. Tools can assist the workflow, but the employer or authorized recruitment source remains the reference for eligibility, availability and application instructions.

Does “verified” guarantee the job is still open right now?+

No. It records the latest check performed by HD Careers. Employers can close or change a role afterward, so candidates should confirm the source again before applying.

Does HD Careers submit applications for candidates?+

No. HD Careers provides job information and links. Applications are completed on the employer or authorized recruitment platform.
