What this snapshot says

Within the active HD Careers database, the most frequently represented employer is HCLTech, the most common location tag is Bengaluru, and the most frequently listed skill phrase is Problem solving. These observations can change as jobs are added or expire.

Fresher-tagged listings: 39. Experienced listings: 17. Government-tagged listings: 12.

Methodology
Only jobs currently marked active are counted. Company names, locations, categories and skill phrases come from the structured HD Careers job records. We do not estimate national hiring volume from this sample and we do not treat a listing count as the number of vacancies.
