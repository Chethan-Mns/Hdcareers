Independent platform

HD Careers is an independent job-information platform. Company names, trademarks and logos belong to their respective owners. Listing a company or opportunity does not imply sponsorship, endorsement or affiliation.

Job accuracy and availability

We aim to summarize official job information accurately, but employers can edit or close postings at any time. The employer's official careers page is the final source for current eligibility, compensation, deadlines and application availability.

Salary and eligibility

When an employer does not disclose salary, batch or another requirement, HD Careers should display it as not disclosed or not specified rather than present an estimate as an official fact. Candidates should verify every important requirement before applying.

Recruitment fraud

HD Careers does not charge candidates for job applications. Be cautious of anyone requesting payment, OTPs, passwords or bank information in exchange for a job or interview. Use the employer's official recruiting channels whenever possible.

Corrections

If you believe a listing is incorrect or closed, report it through our Contact page so it can be reviewed.
