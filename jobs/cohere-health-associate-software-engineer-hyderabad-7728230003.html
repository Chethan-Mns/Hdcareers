Job Overview

Cohere Health is hiring an Associate Software Engineer in Hyderabad for its Clinical Intelligence team. The role focuses on building cloud-based healthcare applications and AI-driven automation, with work spanning databases, backend services, APIs and frontend experiences.

Eligibility

Bachelor's degree or higher in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent experience. The role lists 0-3 years of full-time software development lifecycle experience, proficiency in Java or Python, exposure to React and JavaScript, familiarity with version control, unit testing and CI/CD, cloud development on AWS or Azure, and relational or NoSQL databases.

  • 4Work across the stack from databases and backend services to frontend applications
  • 5Collaborate with product, design and engineering teammates
  • 6Support releases and production systems as part of the software development lifecycle

Who Should Apply?

Early-career software engineers with a relevant technical degree or equivalent experience who are comfortable learning across backend, frontend and cloud technologies should review this role. The official posting allows 0-3 years of experience.
