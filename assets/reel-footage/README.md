# Reusable HD Careers Instagram Reel footage

The daily Reel workflow looks for original or rights-cleared vertical MP4/MOV clips in this directory.

Upload HD_Careers_Reusable_Office_Footage.mp4 once, or other owned generic office clips. The Reel Factory automatically selects a clip based on the verified job ID, loops it, overlays the company logo above the role, burns in correctly synchronized captions, and outputs a new Reel.

Requirements:
- 9:16 portrait; 720x1280 or higher recommended
- 5-20 seconds each, motion footage preferred
- no speaker/third-party copyrighted music in the video (the editor replaces narration)
- no employer-specific buildings or branded signage that would misrepresent a different employer
- verify permission for reuse across promotional social-media posts

Do not commit unlicensed stock footage, personal data or API tokens. Clips are one-time reusable production inputs. Video assets attached in a ChatGPT conversation do not sync to GitHub automatically.
