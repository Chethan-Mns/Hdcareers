About HD Careers

Last updated: 29 September 2026

What we do

HD Careers is an independent job-information website for candidates in India. We organize openings such as fresher jobs, experienced roles, internships, apprenticeships, remote opportunities, walk-ins and government recruitment so candidates can understand the basic requirements before visiting the employer's official application page.

How listings are prepared

Our goal is to use the employer's official careers page or an authorized recruitment portal as the primary source. HD Careers summarizes the role in original wording and highlights the company, role, location, experience, qualification, skills and responsibilities supported by the source. We do not intentionally invent salary, batch eligibility, hiring volume or experience requirements when the employer has not published them.

Verification and freshness

Job availability can change quickly. Listings display a verification date and candidates should always re-check the official employer page immediately before applying. When we confirm that an application is closed, the listing can be marked expired instead of being presented as an active vacancy.

Independence

HD Careers is not a recruitment agency and is not affiliated with the companies listed unless a page explicitly says otherwise. We do not charge candidates to access a job post or to apply for jobs linked from this website.

Corrections

If you find an expired link, incorrect eligibility detail or another issue, please report it through our Contact page. We use correction reports to keep listings more accurate.
